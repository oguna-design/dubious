About Me

I've spent the last five years helping small Nairobi businesses look like the brands they want to become — cafes, salons, consultants, and shops. Most of them had a logo and a dream and not much else.

My job is to turn that into a system: a look, a voice, and a website that does the selling while they sleep. No jargon, no fluff — just design that earns trust and brings in customers.
